# Human academic citation review

Review after substantive drafting and again after renumbering or source changes.
Use the Bluebook edition required by your program. This repository does not
supply that manual or certify compliance. Formatting cannot determine whether
an authority supports a proposition or remains current.

1. **Identify the candidate.** Record source/Git revision, manifest, reference
   export hash, DOCX/PDF hashes, review date, required manual edition and reviewer
   in a private log. Preserve edited Word copies outside version control.
2. **Read the authority.** Check each proposition against the cited passage in
   context: quotations, paraphrases, limitations, procedural posture and actual
   support. A correct-looking citation does not establish support.
3. **Check currency separately.** Verify history/treatment and controlling
   status, amendments/effective dates, editions/supplements, withdrawn or
   superseded bar opinions and web availability. Record the service/source and
   date actually checked. The build performs no citator or legal research search.
4. **Verify metadata and pinpoints.** Check type, jurisdiction, court, date/year,
   reporter/volume/first page, code/subsection, source edition, author/title,
   opinion number, URL and access date. Check abbreviations and each pinpoint
   against the actual source and applicable rules/tables.
5. **Review academic form.** Check contextual roman/italic case names, italic
   signals, small capitals, punctuation, signal meaning/order, authority order,
   parentheticals and needed subsequent history. Check each source subtype;
   the implemented matrix covers only bounded forms.
6. **Resolve every diagnostic.** Read citations.json.warnings. Trace every Id.
   to the eligible previous authority/pinpoint and every supra to its first note.
   Resolve author/title ambiguity. Full-form fallback is a review item, not proof
   that a short form is prohibited. Typed note numbers will not safely renumber.
7. **Inspect both final formats.** In Word confirm editable/native notes,
   continuous numbering, connected references and typography. Inspect every PDF
   page for placement, split notes, glyphs, wrapping, whitespace, rules and page
   numbers. An edited DOCX no longer automatically matches the generated PDF.
8. **Check the guide independently.** Verify 30–35 double-spaced pages and Times
   New Roman in the submitted file. Review the approximate 7,300–8,500 main-text
   word range excluding notes. Treat 100–175 notes as guidance, not a quota.
   Confirm unspecified size, margins and note spacing with the program. Word
   totals do not guarantee pages.

Changes to an authority, proposition, order or reference export invalidate the
affected review. Rebuild/recheck and preserve the reviewed copy with evidence.
The software's separate exact-commit Claude Code/Grok Build merge approvals do
not replace human legal citation review.
